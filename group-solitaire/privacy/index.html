Group Solitaire Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Happy Turnip Games ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you play Group Solitaire (the "Game"). By playing the Game, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

1. Who We Are

Happy Turnip Games is the developer of the Game and the controller of the information described in this policy. You can contact us at happyturnipgames@protonmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details, and the Game does not offer accounts that use them.

Information created when you play:

  • Anonymous account identifier. When you first play, the Game creates a random account identifier for your browser. It is not linked to any email address, social media account, or other account.
  • Player name. You choose a two-word player name from word lists provided by the Game (for example, "Quiet Otter"). Free-text names are not possible.
  • Location flag (optional). If you choose one in Settings, the country flag you pick. The Game never detects your location; it only shows the flag you select, and you can change it to None at any time.
  • Game progress. Daily Challenge results (the dates you completed them and whether you did so on the day), achievements earned, card styles unlocked, and gameplay totals such as games won and cards turned over.
  • Matchmaking information. While you are playing, the Game periodically records that you are online and which players you are seated with, so that it can seat you with other players.

Information collected automatically:

  • IP address and technical information. Like any online service, our service providers receive your IP address and basic technical information (such as browser type) when the Game connects to them. See Section 5 for how live boards share your IP address with other players.

Information stored on your device:

  • The Game saves your settings (such as text size and card back), a copy of your progress, and the sign-in information for your anonymous account in your browser's storage. The Game does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to operate the Game, specifically to:

  • save your progress and settings;
  • seat you with other players and show live boards;
  • display your player name, achievement rank, and location flag (if you chose one) to players you are seated with;
  • maintain the security and reliability of the Game.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (EEA and UK Players)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process your information because it is necessary to provide the Game you have chosen to play (performance of a contract) and, for security and reliability, on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the Game working.

5. How Information Is Shared

  • Other players. Players you are seated with can see your player name, your achievement rank, your location flag (if you chose one), and your game as you play it, including your cards, moves, and pointer. The Game has no chat.
  • Direct connections. Live boards connect your browser directly to the browsers of players you are seated with. As with any direct internet connection, those players' browsers receive your IP address. If a network blocks direct connections, the live board is passed through a relay server instead.
  • Service providers. We use the following providers to run the Game:
    • Supabase stores your anonymous account, player name, location flag (if you chose one), progress, and matchmaking information.
    • Cloudflare operates the connection services that help browsers connect to each other (STUN) and, when a direct connection is blocked, relay live boards between them (TURN). It receives your IP address and, when relaying, the encrypted live board data.
    These providers process information on our behalf and may keep short-term technical logs, including IP addresses.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. International Transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the one you live in, including the United States. Where required, they rely on legal safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

7. Data Retention

We keep your account, player name, and progress until you ask us to delete them. Matchmaking records are kept only as long as needed to seat players and are deleted within 30 days.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to receive a copy of it. To make a request, email happyturnipgames@protonmail.com with your player name and the approximate date you last played. Because accounts are anonymous, we may not be able to locate your information without these details.

Clearing your browser's data for the Game disconnects your browser from its account and removes the copy saved on your device.

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience, including children. We do not ask for personal information, the Game has no chat, and player names can only be chosen from our word lists. We collect only the limited technical information described in this policy, which is needed to operate the Game. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Third-Party Websites

If you play the Game on a website that hosts it, such as CrazyGames or Poki, that website's own privacy policy also applies to your use of it.

11. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls that allow each account to read and change only its own data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at happyturnipgames@protonmail.com.
